INTUBATALK · ICU CLINICAL SYSTEM

Privacy Policy

What this system stores, who can see it, how long it is kept — and what it never does.

Who we are

INTUBATALK is a bedside communication system for intubated and non-verbal ICU patients: a patient tablet, a nurse dashboard, and a printable backup board. This policy explains the data handled by the software in plain language. Hospital IT remains responsible for the environment the system runs in.

What we store

  • Patient name and bed number — entered at the bedside, shown to the nurse who responds.
  • Room codes — four-digit codes used to pair a tablet with a dashboard.
  • Requests and messages — tiles, spelled words, pain scores, and blink answers the patient sends.
  • Acknowledgements — which nurse responded, and when.
  • Nurse profiles — the nurse's name and unit, kept only on the nurse's own device.

On the nurse's device the request feed is also cached in browser storage so the dashboard survives a brief network drop. Clearing the browser storage removes it.

The bedside camera (eye-blink mode)

Eye-blink mode can open the bedside camera so the nurse can watch the patient's eyes live on the tablet screen. Important limits:

  • The camera only starts after explicit permission granted on the device, and only while the blink panel is open.
  • The video stream is processed entirely on the device. It is never recorded, never uploaded, and never sent to the nurse dashboard or any server.
  • Closing the eye-blink panel or the tablet's browser turns the camera off immediately.
  • The camera is an optional aid — the tactile one-blink / two-blink buttons always work without it.

Who can see what

  • The patient's tablet shows only that patient's own information.
  • The nurse dashboard shows only the beds that nurse added with a room code.
  • Anyone who knows a room code could connect to that bed — codes are meant for bedside pairing, not secrecy. Hospital staff should treat the network as a shared clinical environment.
  • Browser notifications and alarms are device-local; no alerts are emailed or texted.

How long data is kept

Requests exist only for the current session: once acknowledged and handled, the feed clears when the patient starts a new session or the cache is cleared. There is no archive, no export, and no medical record integration.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent, or share patient data with anyone.
  • Track patients or staff across sites, or use advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Record or upload camera or microphone video or audio.
  • Replace clinical judgment — this tool relays messages; alarms are a supplement to, not a substitute for, standard ICU monitoring.

Your choices

  • Use Start over on the patient tablet to wipe the session and begin fresh.
  • Deny or revoke camera permission in browser settings at any time.
  • Skip Emergency-push prompts the first time they appear — the system still works fully without them.

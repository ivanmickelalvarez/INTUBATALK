Eye-blink answers
Eye-blink protocol: one blink means yes, two blinks mean no. The nurse asks a question and watches my eyes.

Works together with the INTUBATALK tablet app: patient tablet, Wi-Fi, nurse dashboard. Suction, breathing, toilet and pain are high priority and ring an alarm on the nurse side.
